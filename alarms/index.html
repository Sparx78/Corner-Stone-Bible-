Morning Verses

Wake to the word, read by Voxly

Make one your iPhone alarm

  1. Tap Save under the verse you want, then choose Download. The file goes to Files › Downloads.
  2. Open GarageBand (Apple’s free app; get it from the App Store if it is not on your phone). Tap +, choose Audio Recorder, then tap the tracks button (the bricks icon) at the top.
  3. Tap the loop button (top right), then Files › Browse items from the Files app, and pick the verse. Hold it and drag it into the track, to the very start.
  4. So the whole verse is kept: tap the small + at the right end of the ruler above the track, tap Section A, and turn on Automatic.
  5. Tap the ▾ button (top left) and choose My Songs. Hold the song, then tap Share › Ringtone. Name it, for example “New every morning”, and tap Export.
  6. Open Clock › Alarms, tap your alarm, then Sound. Your verse is at the top of the list under Ringtones.

The alarm repeats the verse until you stop it, with a short pause between each reading. You only need to do this once for each verse.
